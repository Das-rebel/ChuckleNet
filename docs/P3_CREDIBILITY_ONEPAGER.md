### The research that earned this email

We have completed a controlled study showing that frozen speech representations predict listener reactions up to **15 seconds ahead**, with information the transcript does not contain — a finding that survives:

- onset-bleed stratification (laughter starting ≥2.5 s after the context ends)
- comedian-disjoint folds (no same-comedian leakage)
- cross-lab protocol replication on the public TIC-TALK dataset

The finding is published as a **17-row reproducible results log** on GitHub, with pre-registered analysis plans and committed JSON artifacts for every row.
